We begin our journey in a pink and snowy planet...

not too many creatures roam here.. during the night and day its all the same, an eerie quietness fills this cold planet. after what seemed like 40 days and 40 nights of silence, a lonely traveler appears. she leaves behind a trail, little webbed feet have wandered for god knows how long. her name is magnolia. she is a quest to find the traces of her family. she needs to find her colony.
